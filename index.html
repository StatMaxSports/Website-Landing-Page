How It Works

Stat Max applies computer vision and machine learning directly to match footage to automatically detect, label, and quantify in-game events. By minimising the human effort to capture a match, our costs to analyse a game are greatly reduced which allows us to provide high-level performance analytics accessible at every level of sport.
